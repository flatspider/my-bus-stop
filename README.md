## Bauhaus Bus

Bauhaus Bus is a bus stop display app with a more deliberate visual treatment than the usual transit dashboard.

## Demo

![Bauhaus Bus demo](./docs/assets/bauhaus-bus-demo.gif)

Tap the bus stop in the top left corner to search for your bus stop, or open Settings to scan a QR code at your physical stop.

## Runtime Data Policy

Runtime/search-serving artifacts:

- `data/stops-search-index.v1.json`
